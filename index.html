WHO WE ARE

About Our Club

The Rotaract Club of Karatina University is a community of young people committed to creating positive change through service, leadership, friendship and professional development.

We bring together students who want to make a difference both within the university and in the wider community. Through meaningful projects, fellowship and leadership opportunities, we learn, connect and serve together.
